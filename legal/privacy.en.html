Ha!! Buggy is a game for players of all ages, including children. We collect as little as possible. This page explains what the game and the services inside it collect, why, and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, write to {EMAIL}.

1. Age screen and child mode

The first time you open the game, we ask for your year of birth. We only keep the age group on your device, not the date. Players under 13 (or under the age of digital consent in their country), and players who do not answer, use child mode:

  • ads are never personalized and are marked as directed to children;
  • the advertising identifier is not used;
  • publishing levels online and choosing a public name are turned off unless a parent unlocks them.

2. What stays on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, skins, settings) and the levels you make are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. In child mode, ads follow Google's Families requirements: they are not personalized and use no advertising ID. Adults in the EEA/UK are asked for consent through Google's consent form and can change their choice in Settings → Privacy options. See how Google uses data from apps.

4. Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We receive only a confirmation that you bought it, so we can turn ads off. We never see your payment details.

5. Achievements and leaderboards

If you sign in to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center, your gamer name, achievements and scores are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies.

6. Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a level's QR code. The image is processed on your device and is never stored or sent.

7. Online features (only if you use them)

  • Sharing levels: if you publish a level, its design and the display name you choose become public. Don't use your real name.
  • Player ID: online features use a random ID created by the game. It is not linked to your device's advertising ID.
  • Gameplay statistics: we record anonymous events (for example "level 2-3 finished") with that random ID to balance difficulty and fix bugs.
  • Reports: if you report a level, we keep the report to moderate content.
  • Optional account: if an adult links an email address to keep progress across devices, the email is used only for sign-in.

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described here, and we never sell it. A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to review or delete their child's online data, or to stop further collection, by writing to {EMAIL}. We will answer within 30 days.

9. Keeping and deleting data

Statistics are kept for up to 90 days in raw form, then only as totals. Published levels stay online until you or we remove them. To delete everything linked to your player ID, follow the steps on our data deletion page.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the GDPR in Europe, the CCPA in California, COPPA in the United States, or Togo's personal data protection law), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and you can object to some uses. Contact us and we will help.

11. Security and transfers

Data sent online is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our service providers may process data in other countries, with appropriate safeguards.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell players in the game's update notes.
