This page explains how to delete your data from Ha!! Buggy, the game published by {CO} on Google Play and the App Store. Parents can make any of these requests for their child.

Data on your device

Progress, coins, skins, settings and the levels you made are stored only on your device.

  1. To reset the game but keep it installed: open Settings → Reset progress.
  2. To delete everything on the device: uninstall Ha!! Buggy.

Online data

If you used online features (sharing levels in the community, a display name, an optional linked email), ask us to delete them:

  1. Email {EMAIL} with the subject Delete my data.
  2. Include your display name, or the share code of one of your levels, so we can find your player ID. Send it from the linked email address if you added one.
  3. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What we delete

DataWhat happens
Player ID, display name, linked emailDeleted
Levels you published, likes, scoresDeleted from the community and leaderboards
Gameplay statistics linked to your player IDDeleted (raw statistics are kept for at most 90 days anyway)
Reports you sentYour player ID is removed from them
Totals with no link to you (for example "1,000 plays of level 2-3")Kept, because they can't identify anyone

Data held by others

  • Purchases: receipts are kept by Google Play or Apple under their own rules.
  • Ads: Google AdMob data is controlled by Google. Adults can reset or delete their advertising ID in the phone's settings and manage choices at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Achievements: Google Play Games and Game Center profiles are managed in your Google or Apple account.

More details are in our Privacy policy.
