Purchases

I bought Remove ads but I still see ads

Open Settings → Remove ads and tap Restore. Use the same Google or Apple account you bought it with. The purchase works on every device signed in to that account.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store: Google Play (Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history) or Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). If the store can't help, write to us.

Progress

My progress is gone

Progress is saved on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it, and we can't restore it from our side.

Can I start over?

Yes: Settings → Reset progress. Your Remove ads purchase is kept.

Playing

I'm stuck on a level

Every level can be solved. Tap Watch a ghost run to see a winning route, or Stuck? Skip to move on and come back later.

The game feels slow or too flashy

In Settings, lower Quality for older phones and turn on Reduced motion to remove screen shake and flashes.

Is the game in French?

Not yet: the game text is in English. The website and store pages are in English and French.

Levels and sharing

How do I share a level?

In the Level Editor, open your level and tap Share to get a code or a QR code. Friends add it with Import a code or Scan QR.

I saw a level or name that shouldn't be there

Email us its share code with the subject "Report". We review it, usually within 2 working days.

Privacy

Read the Privacy policy, or delete your data. Ad privacy choices are in Settings → Privacy options (shown where the law requires it).
