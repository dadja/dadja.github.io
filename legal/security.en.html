Ha!! Buggy is played by people of every age, including children. This page explains how we keep players and their data safe, and what to do if you find a problem.

Keeping data safe

  • We collect very little. Your progress, settings and levels stay on your device. Details are in the Privacy policy.
  • Encrypted connections. Every online request from the game uses HTTPS.
  • No passwords to steal. Online features work with a random player ID created by the game. If an adult links an email address, sign-in uses one-time codes sent by email.
  • Limited access. Only the {CO} team can reach our servers, with personal accounts and two-step sign-in.
  • Trusted providers. Purchases go through Google Play and the App Store; we never see card numbers.

Keeping children safe

  • Child mode after a neutral age question: non-personalized, family-rated ads only, and no advertising ID.
  • No chat and no private messages. Players can't contact each other in the game.
  • Moderated level sharing. Shared levels and names follow our rules. Reported content is reviewed, usually within 2 working days, and removed when it breaks them.
  • Parents stay in control. Online publishing is off in child mode unless an adult unlocks it, and parents can ask us to delete their child's data at any time.

Report a problem

Seen an inappropriate level, name or ad? Send its share code or a screenshot to {EMAIL} with the subject "Report". We reply within 2 working days.

Report a security issue

If you think you found a security weakness in the game, our servers or this website, email {EMAIL} with the subject "Security" and the steps to reproduce it.

  • We confirm receipt within 5 working days and keep you informed until it's fixed.
  • Please don't access, change or delete other players' data, run denial-of-service tests or publish the issue before it's fixed.
  • Acting in good faith under these rules, you won't face legal action from us, and we'll gladly thank you publicly if you wish.

Our contact details are also published in the standard security.txt file.

Avoid scams

  • The only official downloads are Google Play and the App Store. Don't install Ha!! Buggy from other websites.
  • We never ask for passwords, codes or payments by email, message or social media.
  • There is nothing to buy except Remove ads, inside the game. Offers of "free coins" or "unlock everything" are fake.
